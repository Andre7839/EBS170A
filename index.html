01 · Engineering ethics case simulator

Review the situation, choose a response, and explain your professional reasoning. The feedback is formative; it is not a substitute for instructor evaluation.

Case: Pressure to approve a design
A project manager asks you to approve a machine-guarding design before a safety review is complete. The project is behind schedule. You have noticed that a moving component may remain accessible during operation.

Reflection: Could schedule pressure, financial incentives, or organizational hierarchy create a conflict of interest? What documentation and escalation steps would be appropriate?

03 · Codes and standards navigator

Learn how to identify potentially relevant standards. Selecting a topic provides a starting point for research—not a compliance determination.

Examples of organizations: OSHA, NFPA, ASME, ASCE, ACI, AISC, IEEE, ASTM, and ISO. Always verify the adopted edition, jurisdictional requirements, scope, and authoritative text.

04 · Engineering failure investigation

Separate evidence from assumptions, identify contributing factors, and recommend corrective actions.

Case: Repeated pump shaft failures
A process-water pump has experienced two shaft failures in six months. Maintenance records show increased vibration before both failures. The pump was recently operated outside its original design flow range. No complete alignment record is available after the last maintenance activity.
